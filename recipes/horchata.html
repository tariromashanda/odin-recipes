Horchata

Description: A simplified version of a light and refreshing Mexican drink

Ingredients

  • 2 sticks of cinnamon bark
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  • 1l rice milk
  • 1.5l almond milk
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup

Instructions

  1. Add 500ml rice milk, 500ml almond milk and the cinnamon bark to a pot
  2. Bring pot to boil and reduce tempreture to low heat and allow to simmer for 20 mintues.
  3. Remove pot from heat and allow to sit for another 20 mintues
  4. Add the remaining almond milk and rice milk to the mixture
  5. Add the vanilla extract and maple syrup and mix until well combined
  6. Pour the mixture into a jug and place in the fridge until cool
  7. Serve over a glass of ice nd sprinkle some cinnamon ontop of the drink
