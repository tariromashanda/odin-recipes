Gluten-free Pannekoeke

Description: South African style pancakes rolled and filled with cinnamon sugar

Ingredients

Pannekoeke Batter

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 500ml milk
  • 125ml water
  • 120 grams gluten free all purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tbsp sugar

Cinnamon Sugar:

  • 60g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Mix together the cinnamon and suagr and set aside
  2. Whisk together the eggs, vanilla extract in a medium sized bowl.
  3. Add the milk and water, and whisk until well combined.
  4. In a seprate bowl, mix the flour, baking powder and salt together.
  5. Gradually add the wet mixture to the dry mixture and whisk until well combined
  6. Whisk in the melted butter and let the batter rest for 15 minutes
  7. Add a little bit of oil to a large large nonstick frying pan and bring the pan to a medium-high heat
  8. Scoop about 1/3 of batter into the pan, swirling to ensure it covers the whole pan.
  9. Cook until the batter until the edges begin to pull away from the pan, flip and cook for another few seconds
  10. Transfer the pancake to a plate and sprinkle with some cinnamon sugar before rolling and settig aside
